The Beginning

From Computer Science Student to Systems Builder

Fidel Ouma's path into technology started the way most serious ones do — with curiosity and a lot of late nights. He studied for a BSc in Computer Science at Laikipia University, where his interest gradually narrowed from general programming into the specific craft of building complete, working systems: databases, servers, and the interfaces people actually use.

What set his approach apart early on was a refusal to stay purely academic. While many classmates focused on theory, Fidel kept building — small tools, experiments, and eventually full applications — because he wanted to understand software the way a mechanic understands an engine: by taking it apart and putting it back together himself.

That hands-on instinct became the foundation of everything that followed. He didn't just want to write code; he wanted to ship products that solved real problems for real businesses.

Building Serina Systems

A One-Man Engineering Team, Scaling by Reputation

Serina Systems was founded in Nakuru, Kenya, as a software development and digital marketing agency — and for most of its life, Fidel has been the sole developer behind it. Every backend, every deployment, every late-stage bug fix has passed through his hands directly, which is precisely why clients keep coming back: there's no hand-off, no dilution, just one person who understands the whole system from top to bottom.

His technical foundation is built on Node.js, Express, and PostgreSQL for backends, paired with clean, dependency-light HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the frontend. It's a stack chosen for reliability and speed of delivery over trend-chasing — software that works well today and is still easy to maintain years from now.

The client list reflects that philosophy: construction and engineering firms, hospitality venues, retail shops, and educational platforms across Kenya, each with a working, deployed system built from the ground up. Along the way, Fidel has also taken on the technical groundwork that most agencies outsource — SEO setup, PageSpeed optimization, and M-Pesa payment integrations — because he sees those as part of building software that actually performs, not just software that looks good in a pitch.
