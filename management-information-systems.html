Management Information Systems Overview

A Management Information System (MIS) gathers the data your organisation produces every day, keeps it in one secure place and turns it into clear reports, so managers can make decisions quickly and with confidence. At Serina Systems we build custom MIS platforms that replace paper files, scattered spreadsheets and guesswork with one system your whole team can rely on.

Every system is designed around the way your business actually works. We build the back end with Node.js, Express and PostgreSQL, and a fast, clean web interface that opens on any phone, tablet or computer, with no software to install. Staff get the access they need, managers get live dashboards and reports, and your data stays organised and protected.

  • Built around your processes
  • Role-based user access
  • Live dashboards and reports
  • One secure central database
  • Works on any device
  • Training and ongoing support

Benefits With Our Service

Working Process

A clear six-step roadmap takes your system from first conversation to a team that uses it with confidence.

We study how your organisation works today and agree exactly what the system must do.

  • Free consultation and workflow review
  • List of users, roles and reports needed
  • Scope, timeline and fixed quote

We plan the database and screens so the system is simple to use and ready to grow.

  • Database structure and data relationships
  • Screen layouts and navigation
  • Your approval before building starts

We build the system in stages, so you see real progress and can guide it along the way.

  • Back end, database and secure login
  • Responsive web interface
  • Progress shared on a private preview link

We test every feature with realistic data and move your existing records safely into the new system.

  • Feature, security and device testing
  • Import of existing spreadsheets and records
  • Your review and sign-off

We put the system live and train your team so everyone is confident from day one.

  • Hosting, domain and SSL set-up
  • Hands-on training for each user role
  • User guide and handover

After launch we stay close, fixing issues quickly and adding features as your needs change.

  • Backups, updates and fast fixes
