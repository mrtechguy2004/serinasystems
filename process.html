Our Roadmap

Six steps to a website that works

From the first conversation to ongoing support, this is how we turn your idea into a finished, fast and reliable product.

Step 01

Discovery & Consultation

We start by listening. We learn about your business, your customers, your competitors and what you want your website or system to achieve.

  • Free consultation call or meeting
  • A clear written project brief
Typically 1 - 3 days
01
Step 02

Planning & Proposal

We map out the pages, features and technology your project needs, then send a transparent proposal with the scope, price and delivery timeline.

  • Sitemap and feature list
  • Fixed quote and project schedule
Typically 2 - 4 days
02
Step 03

Design & Prototype

We design the look and feel around your brand, mobile-first, so you can see exactly how your site will appear before a single line of final code is written.

  • Mobile-first page designs
  • Feedback rounds until you are happy
Typically 3 - 7 days
03
Step 04

Development

Our developers bring the approved design to life with clean, fast and secure code, and build in the features, forms and databases your business needs.

  • Fast, secure, search-friendly code
  • Progress updates on a private preview link
Typically 1 - 4 weeks
04
Step 05

Testing & Review

Before anything goes live we test on phones, tablets and desktops, check speed and security, and walk you through the finished work for your final approval.

  • Cross-device and browser testing
  • Your sign-off before launch
Typically 2 - 5 days
05
Step 06

Launch & Support

We take your project live, set up hosting and your domain, and show you how everything works. After launch we stay close with maintenance and friendly support.

  • Smooth, stress-free go-live
  • Ongoing maintenance and support
