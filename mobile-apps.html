Mobile App Overview

Your customers live on their phones, so your business should too. Serina Systems designs and builds mobile apps that are simple to use, quick to load and genuinely useful, helping you serve customers better, take bookings and orders, and run your operations from anywhere.

We start with your goals and your users, design a clean interface you can try before we build, and then develop a secure, reliable app with a back end that keeps everything in sync. We build native Android apps with Kotlin as well as cross-platform apps that can reach both Android and iPhone, depending on what suits your budget and audience.

Working Process

A clear six-step roadmap takes your app from first idea to the app store.

We learn who your users are and what the app must achieve, then define the features that matter most for a first release.

  • Free consultation and app brief
  • Competitor and user research
  • Feature list, platform choice and fixed quote

We map the user journey and design clean, thumb-friendly screens, then share a clickable prototype you can try on your phone.

  • Wireframes and user flows
  • Branded screen designs
  • Clickable prototype and feedback rounds

We build your app in short stages, connecting it to a secure back end, database and any services it needs.

  • Native or cross-platform build
  • API, database and admin tools
  • Test builds shared as the app takes shape

We test on real devices and different screen sizes, fix bugs and tune performance and battery use before release.

  • Real-device testing
  • Performance, security and usability checks
  • Your review and sign-off

We prepare your store listing and screenshots and guide the app through submission to Google Play, and the App Store where needed.

  • Store listing and assets
  • Submission and review support
  • Launch-day monitoring

After launch we monitor crashes and feedback, keep the app compatible with new phone updates and add features as you grow.

  • Bug fixes and compatibility updates
  • Feature updates and improvements
  • Growth and marketing support
